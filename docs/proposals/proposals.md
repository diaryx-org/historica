# Proposals

Documents that argue for a change, and may lose the argument. A proposal
that wins becomes a [decision](../decisions/index.md) when it is built, and
the decision is the authority from then on; the proposal stays here, with its
outcome in `status` and a **Status** section at the top, because the
reasoning is the value even when it lost.

`status` takes `draft`, `accepted`, `implemented`, `deferred`, or `rejected`,
and nothing else.
